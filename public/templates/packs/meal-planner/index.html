How to fill this template

Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for each day. Build a shopping list from your plan and note prep batches. Print and stick on the fridge.

  • Plan protein first — then build sides around it.
  • Repeat 2 dinners mid-week to cut cooking time.
  • Shop from the list only — stick to budget.

Filled example

Mon dinner: Grilled chicken + rice + salad
Shop: chicken, rice, greens, olive oil
Prep Sunday: cook grains, chop veg
